TrapWin HALO - macOS install (Apple Silicon & Intel)

1. Copy "TrapWin HALO.vst3" to   /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
2. Copy "TrapWin HALO.component" to   /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (Logic, GarageBand)
   Tip: in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the path.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
