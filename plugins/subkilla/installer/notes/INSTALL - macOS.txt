TrapWin SUB KILLA - macOS install (Apple Silicon & Intel)

1. Copy "TrapWin SUB KILLA.vst3" to   /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
2. Copy "TrapWin SUB KILLA.component" to   /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (Logic, GarageBand)
   Tip: in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the path.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins. SUB KILLA loads as an instrument (Logic: Software Instrument > AU Instruments > TrapWin).
