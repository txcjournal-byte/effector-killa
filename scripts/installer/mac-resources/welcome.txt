TrapWin BEND DEMON

Write plain melodies. Hear them bend.

BEND DEMON hosts your MPE synth (Serum 2, Vital, Pigments, Phase Plant ...) and turns the
notes of your piano roll into expressive MPE: glides, 808 dives, vibrato, trills and more.

This installer copies the VST3, the Audio Unit and the standalone app. Restart your DAW and
rescan plug-ins afterwards.
