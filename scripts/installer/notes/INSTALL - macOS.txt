TrapWin BEND DEMON 2.0.0 - macOS installation (Apple Silicon & Intel)
=====================================================================

The plugin is signed and notarized by Apple.

1. Unzip the download (double-click it).
2. In Finder press Shift+Cmd+G and go to:  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/
   - copy "TrapWin BEND DEMON.vst3"      into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   - copy "TrapWin BEND DEMON.component" into  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/   (AU - Logic Pro, GarageBand)
   (You can also use ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/... for your user only.)
   Updating from 1.0.0? Delete the old "BEND DEMON.vst3" and "BEND DEMON.component" there first.
3. Optional: copy "TrapWin BEND DEMON.app" (standalone) into /Applications.
4. Open your DAW and rescan plugins.
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > "Find installed plugins", then tick TrapWin BEND DEMON (Generators).
   Logic Pro checks AU plugins automatically on start.

Getting started (2.0)
- Add BEND DEMON to a channel. It already has a demo pattern and a built-in sound:
  press play in your DAW (or the PLAY button in the plugin) and you hear slides right away.
- Draw your own lines with the pencil: one line = one note that can slide anywhere.
- Pick a sound at the top (SOUND), or load your own synth. Synths with MPE (Serum 2, Vital,
  Pigments, Phase Plant, Surge XT ...) slide every note on its own; other synths get mono slides
  automatically (set their pitch-bend range to the value in the settings menu, default 48).
- LIVE mode (top bar) = the 1.x behaviour: your piano-roll notes are bent by the gestures.
